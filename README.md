# Working notes on OpenAI's quasi-Riemann hypothesis papers, and generalizations to CM newforms

> These are working notes, generated by Claude and Codex to help me read the papers on the
> quasi-Riemann hypothesis that OpenAI released
> ([github.com/openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints)).
> We also prove analogous zero-free strips for the L-functions of the elliptic curves y² = x³ + d:
> L(E_d, s) ≠ 0 for Re s > 17/12 by the 11/12 method, and for Re s > 11/8 by the 7/8 method.
> In the analytic normalization, with critical line Re s = 1/2, these are Re s > 11/12 and Re s > 7/8.
> — [Michael Rubinstein](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/)

- [Sixth powers in a mean square: an accessible guide to the 11/12 argument for ζ(s)](zeta_11_12_guide.pdf) (PDF, 18 pages)
- [Zeros of a whole family: an accessible guide to the 7/8 argument for ζ(s)](zeta_7_8_guide.pdf) (PDF, 12 pages)
- [A zero-free strip for the L-functions of the elliptic curves y² = x³ + d: the 11/12 method and its extension to 7/8](cm_zero_free_strip.pdf) (PDF, 125 pages; [TeX source](cm_zero_free_strip.tex)).
  This note adapts the two methods to the CM curves y² = x³ + d.
- [Quadratic number fields, with Q(√−3) worked out in detail](quadratic_fields_notes.pdf) (PDF, 33 pages;
  [source and scripts](quadratic_fields_notes/)). Background notes on the arithmetic of ℤ[ω], residue symbols,
  Poisson summation over ℤ[ω], and the cubic theta function of Kubota and Patterson, as used in the 11/12 argument.

All four notes are also posted at <https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/quasi_rh_notes/>.

## Numerical checks of the 11/12 guide

[`zeta_11_12_tests/`](zeta_11_12_tests/) contains the Python scripts behind Section 9 of the 11/12 guide. They test the
identities and parameter inequalities of its Sections 1–8 on finite samples; the analytic bounds get sanity checks
of their size only. Run `python3 zeta_11_12_tests/run_all.py`; this takes about 2 minutes and needs numpy, scipy,
sympy and mpmath. The [README there](zeta_11_12_tests/README.md) explains each check and maps it to the guide's
equation numbers.
